Inclusive Food Literacy

Who This System Helps

Making small, crowded, and technical food labels accessible to everyone.

Elderly People

Overcomes tiny, crowded print on packaging with large readable cards and optional Text-to-Speech audio playback.

Limited-English Users

Breaks language barriers by translating unfamiliar English ingredient lists into native regional and global languages.

Low Food-Label Literacy

Converts complex chemical names and E-numbers (like emulsifiers & acidity regulators) into simple everyday words.

Allergy & Dietary Checking

Instantly flags declared allergens (nuts, dairy, soy, gluten) so shoppers can make safe decisions before buying.
